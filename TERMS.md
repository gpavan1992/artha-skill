## 1. What the Plugin does

Artha is a set of skills and Python scripts that run on your own machine or in your AI assistant's execution environment. They read files you name, fetch public market data and report what it shows: mutual fund NAVs and returns, NSE/BSE prices, reported financials, news and announcements, fund overlap, capital-gains estimates, spend summaries and rules-based screens. Artha has no broker login. It cannot place, modify or cancel orders, and it does not move money.
